Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

The most important points first

LaLeLu is built so that as little data as possible ends up anywhere. Video and sound travel directly and encrypted between your family's devices, there is no user account with us, and the only information stored long-term outside your device (in iCloud) is a small technical record that is automatically deleted after 30 days. On the device itself, the app stores only a few counters (e.g. for the rating prompt) and up to 5 short crash summaries. None of this leaves the device unless you yourself send a feedback report (see Section 9).

No user account · No recording · Deletion after 30 days

What is this about?

LaLeLu is a baby monitor for iPhone, iPad and Mac. This policy describes as honestly and precisely as possible which data arises when you use it, where it goes and what happens to it.

2. How does LaLeLu work — and where does data flow?

2.1 Video and sound of the transmission

Picture and sound are transmitted end-to-end encrypted directly between your family's devices (technology: WebRTC with industry-standard DTLS-SRTP encryption). Only the devices you have paired yourself can see the picture.

If a direct connection is technically not possible, the data stream — still encrypted — is routed through an intermediary server of Cloudflare (TURN relay). Even then, Cloudflare cannot decrypt or read picture and sound. Video or sound is never recorded or stored at any time.

2.2 Sound detection

LaLeLu detects e.g. crying or coughing entirely on the device (Apple's SoundAnalysis). No audio is sent to the cloud or to us and nothing is stored for training purposes.

2.3 Pairing of the devices

For pairing, LaLeLu uses your existing Apple iCloud access (CloudKit). LaLeLu itself has no account of its own, no registration, no password. The pairing key remains with Apple/iCloud and on your devices.

2.4 Connection setup (signaling)

Before a direct connection is made, the devices technically "arrange" it via an intermediary server (Cloudflare Worker). The contents of this arrangement are additionally encrypted (AES-GCM). If this route fails, iCloud serves as a fallback.

2.5 Alerts via notification

If a sound is detected and you are not in the app, LaLeLu can send a notification — via the Cloudflare intermediary to Apple's push service (APNs).

3. What is stored long-term — and where?

3.1 On the intermediary server (Cloudflare)

So that your device can receive notifications, the server stores a small record per parent device:

  • the Apple push token (anonymous device identifier assigned by Apple — no name, no phone number),
  • the information iOS or iPadOS,
  • a timestamp of the last update.

Grouping is done via a randomly generated identifier that is not linked to Apple ID, name or personal details. Automatic deletion after 30 days without an update.

Not stored: names, email, location, picture/sound content, alert history.

For transparency, two items pass through the server unencrypted (plain text), only for the seconds of delivery, without being stored: the device name (e.g. "Moritz's iPhone") and the type of sound (e.g. "coughing"). We state this openly because transparency matters more to us than a flawless image.

3.2 IP addresses

As with any internet service, the IP address is visible to the network service provider (here Cloudflare) when a connection is set up. LaLeLu itself does not store IP addresses. Cloudflare's privacy notices also apply.

3.3 On your device (local)

A local activity history (time, type, detection confidence) remains exclusively on your device, is limited to max. 500 entries / 14 days and is not uploaded.

8. Your rights

Since LaLeLu can also be used in the EU/Germany/Austria, the GDPR applies. Among other things, you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

How to delete your data
Dissolve the pairing in the app and/or uninstall the app. The small push record on the intermediary server expires automatically at the latest 30 days after the last activity. For further requests: contact address from Section 1.

9. Feedback and crash reports

What stays local: The app detects crashes and hangs using Apple's system service MetricKit. Of these, up to 5 short summaries (time, type, app build, system version, short description) are stored only on your device. In addition there are simple counters (e.g. number of connections, setup times) and the data for the rating prompt, also stored only locally on your device. None of this is sent automatically to us or to third parties.

What is sent only when you take action: Via "Report a problem / Feedback" in the app you can generate a feedback report. You see the complete text beforehand and decide yourself whether to send it by email, share it or just copy it. The report contains the app version, device model, system version, your role (baby or parent unit), connection counters and times, error categories and the crash summaries. It does not contain names, pairing links, keys or room identifiers.

Recipient, purpose and legal basis

The sole recipient is the developer (Section 1, moritz.wolf@a1.net). If you send the report by email, the recipient also receives your sender address and anything you write in the email yourself. The purpose is solely to fix errors and improve the app. The legal basis is your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, which you give by deliberately sending the report. Without you sending it, no data is transmitted.

Retention and deletion: Reports and emails received are kept until the matter is resolved and are then deleted. You can request deletion at any time (contact in Section 1). The locally stored summaries and counters disappear when you uninstall the app. The rating prompt itself is displayed by the Apple system dialog; you submit the rating directly to Apple.

10. Changes

If anything about LaLeLu changes in a way that affects your data, we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date above.
